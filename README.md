# BDOAA in the News

News reports and features covering BDOAA's national competitions, training programmes, and Bangladesh's participation and achievements at international astronomy and astrophysics Olympiads.

## Featured coverage

### IOAA 2026 — Individual awards and international teamwork

**The Messenger**

Coverage of Arash Zuhayr Udoy's bronze medal, Md Talha Zobair's honourable mention, and Junaed Labeeb Al Waasi's participation in a winning multinational group. The article introduces all five students and both team leaders, explains the competition, and discusses its educational value.

[Read the article](https://themessenger.news/news.php?slug=bangladesh-shines-at-the-19th-ioaa-bronze-medal-honorable-mention-and-second-consecutive-group-competition-success&lang=en)

### IOAA 2024 — The students behind the achievements

**The Daily Star · 31 August 2024**

Tasdik Ahmed Tonmay and Labib Bin Azad share their experiences after earning a bronze medal and an honourable mention in Brazil. The report also introduces the delegation and BDOAA's preparations for its first IOAA Junior participation.

[Read the article](https://online.thedailystar.net/tech-startup/news/bangladesh-wins-bronze-international-astronomy-and-astrophysics-olympiad-3690826)

### IOAA 2023 — Two silver medals and one bronze

**The Financial Express · 5 September 2023**

Coverage of Bangladesh's results in Poland, alongside an explanation of BDOAA's regional and national competitions, training camps and team selection process.

[Read the article](https://thefinancialexpress.com.bd/education/bangladesh-team-bags-2-silver-and-1-bronze-in-the-international-olympiad-on-astronomy-astrophysics)
